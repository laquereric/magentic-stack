== TWO CONSTRAINTS YOU MUST RECKON WITH ==

CONSTRAINT 1 - GREEN/RED IS A DERIVED BAND, NEVER A STORED STATUS.
"Fully clarified" is not a field somebody sets. In OSI Level 8 Profile 11 (Meaning), actability is DERIVED from five stored closed dimensions (definitionLifecycle, agreement, dispute, formalization, binding) into three bands: explorable, plan-eligible, effect-eligible. Bands are NEVER stored -- an attempt to store one is refused by name (MEANING_BAND_FORBIDDEN).
So a green card is a VIEW of a derivation, not a status column somebody drags into. Design accordingly and be explicit about it:
  - What exactly do green and red mean in terms of the derived bands? Is green "effect-eligible", or "no unmet criteria", or something else? Two colours may be too few for three bands plus an unknown state -- say so if it is.
  - A Kanban board normally implies DRAG-TO-CHANGE-STATUS. Here you cannot drag a card to make it clarified; you must satisfy criteria. How does the board make that honest without feeling broken to a user who expects Kanban affordances?
  - Profile 11 also has EligibilityExplanation, a request-time projection listing criterion identifiers and passing/failing values with IRI references. That is exactly the material for explaining WHY a card is red. Use it. It is a projection, never persisted.

CONSTRAINT 2 - THE ACIA VOCABULARY IS CLOSED AT 19 KINDS.
Every page in this product is an ACIA tree drawn from a closed component vocabulary. The 19 kinds are: PageShell PanelFrame SemanticText StatusBadge MetricStrip ContextBanner DrillDownCard DataList Timeline EvidencePanel DecisionForm ActionControl Disclosure FilterBar TabSet EmptyState RefusalNotice ScopeTrail ReferentBridge.
There is NO Board kind, NO Column kind, NO Card kind, and no drag-and-drop behaviour in the closed behaviorKind set (static disclose filter navigate inspect collect_effect acknowledge confirm).
Answer honestly: can a Kanban board be expressed in the existing 19 kinds -- for example columns as PanelFrame containing DataList, cards as DrillDownCard -- or does it genuinely require new kinds? If it can be expressed, give the exact ACIA tree. If it cannot, say precisely what is missing and propose it as a NUMBERED Profile 9 revision with blast radius, in a separate section, NOT assumed approved. Do not quietly invent a component.
Also note the cross-column highlighting behaviour: 'explore' highlights likely hits in another column. Is that expressible, and under which behaviorKind?

== A DEFECT IN THE SPEC, FOR YOU TO RESOLVE ==
Meaning and Clarification are given the SAME definition text: "makes a term adequate for accountable planning or effect." That is almost certainly a copy-paste slip. Propose a correct, distinct definition for each that preserves the operator's evident intent -- Meaning as "what am I making of this?" and Clarification as "what clarification do I need?" -- and flag the change rather than silently rewriting.
